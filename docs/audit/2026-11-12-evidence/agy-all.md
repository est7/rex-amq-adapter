## SEC-1 Managed mail flag-parsing differential permits gateway bypass across outbound and inbound verbs
- Severity: Critical
- Dimension: Security
- Evidence level: traced
- Evidence: `internal/app/managed.go:42-46, 78-89, 91-133, 165-174, 307, 368, 380-400, 409, 442-465`; `internal/app/gateway.go:40-58, 134-207`; `internal/amq/lifecycle.go:343-351`
- Impact: `takeFlag` (`managed.go:442-465`) scans raw CLI slices without an arity table for `amq`'s valued flags and halts immediately when any element equals `"--"` (`managed.go:445-447`), whereas `amq`'s `flag.FlagSet` consumes `"--"` when it is the value of an earlier valued flag and continues parsing subsequent flags. Every managed pass-through (`gateway.go:191`; `managed.go:79-88, 307, 368, 399, 409`) appends caller-supplied `o.Args` or `rest` after the adapter's own `--root`, `--me`, `--to`, `--id`, `--context`, `--json`, `--agents`, and `--message-ids` flags, and `m.dlq` (`managed.go:381`) discards the stripped slice returned by `takeFlag(rest, "id", true)`. Because Go's `flag.FlagSet` lets later flags overwrite earlier ones, trailing flags hidden from `takeFlag` override the adapter's identity, routing, and provenance-token flags inside `amq`. On `send`/`reply`, overriding `--me`, `--to`, `--context`, and `--json` causes `amq.Post` (`amq/lifecycle.go:344-351`) to see `sent.ID == ""` while delivering an untokenized message that the recipient's `judge` (`gateway.go:47-57`) evaluates against `endpointOf(hdr.From)` and admits across Epics or under another sender's handle. On `read`, `thread`, `dlq read`, and `list`, overriding `--me`, `--id`, `--agents`, or `--message-ids` after `m.readable`/`m.exposed` discloses quarantined or cross-Epic mailbox contents.
- Fix sketch: Replace `takeFlag` raw slice pass-through in `internal/app/managed.go` with per-verb `flag.FlagSet` allowlists covering only permitted non-routing `amq` flags, reject unknown flags and unexpected positional arguments, and build the child `amq` argv solely from parsed struct fields. In `internal/app/gateway.go:47-57`, tighten `judge` once managed mail is universal so untokenized mail claiming a live adapter-adopted handle is quarantined rather than trusted via `hdr.From`.
- Blast radius: `rex-amq-adapter send|reply|read|drain|thread|trace|dlq|list` CLI flag acceptance (`ErrUsage` exit 2 on undocumented `amq` flags); `internal/app/managed.go`, `internal/app/gateway.go`, and `internal/app` gateway tests; no on-disk state or hook protocol change.

## SEC-2 Summon/resume omits `--` before prompt args, hardcodes `--dangerously-skip-permissions` on Antigravity, and under-checks summon grants
